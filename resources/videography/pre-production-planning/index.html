Pre-production & Planning

Why planning your shoot ahead of time produces smoother production and stronger final video.

Plan the Details First

Successful videography begins before the camera rolls. Use pre-production to align creative goals, technical requirements, and scheduling.

  • Shot list — define the scenes, camera angles, and story beats you need.
  • Location plans — scout lighting, sound, and power needs ahead of time.
  • Audio capture — choose the right microphones, placement, and recording workflow for interviews and ambience.

When everyone knows the plan, the crew moves faster, reshoots are minimized, and the edit has stronger material to work with.

Review and adapt

Keep a simple checklist for call sheets, shot orders, and equipment. Revisit the plan on set and adjust as needed while preserving the overall vision.

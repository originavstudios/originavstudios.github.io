About Origin AV Studios

We are an audio-visual integration and creative media production company serving Eastern North Carolina.

Who We Are

Origin AV Studios is a locally-owned audio-visual integration and media production company based in Eastern North Carolina. Since 2023, we've delivered professional-grade AV systems, live sound engineering, photography, and videography to churches, businesses, and event venues across the region.

We combine deep technical expertise with creative vision to design and install reliable AV systems that work flawlessly for our clients.

Our Mission

To deliver professional-quality audio-visual and creative media services that support the client through reliable technology and creative excellence.

Our Values

  • Reliability: Systems and services you can count on
  • Professionalism: Expert knowledge, clear communication, timely delivery
  • Quality: Attention to detail and commitment to excellence in everything we do
  • Partnership: We work collaboratively with clients to understand and exceed their needs
  • Innovation: Staying current with technology to bring the best solutions to our clients

What We Do

We offer a complete range of AV and creative media services:

  • AV Integration: System design, installation, and integration for houses of worship, schools, and corporate environments
  • Audio Engineering: Live sound mixing, recording, and broadcast solutions
  • Photography: Event, portrait, and commercial photography with post-production
  • Videography: Event coverage, promotional videos, interviews, and cinematic production
